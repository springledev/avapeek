# Getting started for families

Someone in your family runs Avapeek and has invited you to see photos of a child. This page explains what happens next. You do not need to install anything technical.

## 1. Open your invite link

The invite is a link sent by message or email. Open it on the phone, tablet or computer you want to use. The page shows whose photos you are joining.

Invite links stop working after **7 days**. If yours has expired, ask the person who sent it for a new one.

## 2. Create your account

Choose your name, enter your email address and pick a password of at least 8 characters.

::: warning Write your password down
Your password is the key to the photos. Nobody can see it or reset it, not even the person who runs Avapeek. Keep it in a password manager or written down somewhere safe at home.
:::

## 3. Wait a moment for access

Right after you join, the album may look empty or show lock icons. That is normal. The photos are encrypted, and an album admin has to open Avapeek and approve you, which shares the key with you from their device. Nothing is shared until they do, so it can take a little while.

The admin sees the name on your account and the album you joined. If they decline, you are removed from the album and need a new invite. That name comes from the Avapeek server, so approval is a person's check and not proof of who you are. If the admin wants to be sure, tell them you have joined in another way, such as a message or a call. A way to compare key fingerprints is planned, not yet available.

If it takes longer than you expected, tap **Remind admins**. You get a notification as soon as you can see the photos, and they appear without reloading the page.

## 4. Put Avapeek on your home screen

Avapeek works in the web browser, but it feels like a normal app once it is on your home screen:

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the site in **Safari**, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the site in **Chrome**, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **Computer:** in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.

On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work after Avapeek has been added to the home screen. You can choose which notifications you get under **Settings → Notifications**.

## What can I do in Avapeek?

You can scroll through the photos, open them full size and leave comments. Depending on what the person who invited you chose, you may also be able to upload photos yourself.

## I forgot my password

Because nobody can reset it, a forgotten password means that account cannot open the photos any more. Ask a family member who still has access to send you a new invite, and create a new account with it. Once they have shared access again, you can see the same photos as before.

## Is this private?

Yes. Photos and comments are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, so the server only stores scrambled data. [How the encryption works](/security) explains this in more detail.
